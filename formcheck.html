Prototype

Tracks your joints while you move, works out which movement pattern you're doing, counts your reps, and checks the cues a camera can actually verify. Everything runs on your own device — the video is never uploaded anywhere.

What this can and can't do It measures geometry — joint positions and angles it can see. It can tell you your knee passed your ankle by a certain amount, or that your hips sagged. It can't see what's in your hands, so it detects the pattern (a squat) rather than the exact lift (a front squat vs a goblet squat) — you confirm that bit. It also can't judge bracing, grip, breathing, or pain. Cues it can't verify are marked as such rather than quietly passed.
